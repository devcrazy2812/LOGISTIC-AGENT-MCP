Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Information We Collect

LogiSwarm AI is committed to privacy and data minimization. We only collect telemetry and operational parameters necessary for automated fleet dispatch, including:

  • Vehicle GPS coordinates, speed vectors, and battery/fuel consumption levels.
  • Consignment metadata (dimensions, weight, cargo category, priority deadlines).
  • Audit logs of autonomous agent deliberations and human operator feedback.
  • Voluntary dispatcher prompts submitted via the conversational interface.

2. AI & Multimodal Data Processing

Our system utilizes Google Gemini models for natural language dialogue and multimodal cargo inspection:

  • No Personal Identifiable Imagery: Cargo damage inspection images are restricted to non-personal physical freight, containers, and shipping labels.
  • Zero Secret Transmission: API keys and secrets are strictly retained server-side. No credential tokens are exposed to the client-side browser context.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage exclusively to save your UI preferences (such as dark mode contrast settings and cookie consent status). We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies or behavioral tracking pixels.

4. Security & Data Protection

All data in transit is protected using Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), Content Security Policy (CSP), and TLS 1.3 encryption. Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool endpoints are isolated via JSON-RPC 2.0 validation.

5. Contact & Data Inquiries

For privacy compliance questions or data deletion requests, contact the LogiSwarm AI Operations Team at privacy@logiswarm.ai or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
